Parks and Recreation — Christmas-Themed Episodes

Parks and Recreation kept its Christmas output small but strong — three episodes, including "Citizen Knope," where the department quietly bands together to find Leslie the perfect gift. All seven seasons stream in the U.S. on Peacock, with individual episodes available to rent or buy. Ratings are each episode's IMDb user score.

Episode Synopsis

"Christmas Scandal" — Season 2, Episode 12

A private meeting between Leslie and disgraced Councilman Bill Dexhart spirals into a full-blown Pawnee media scandal once word gets out, even though nothing actually happened between them. While Leslie scrambles to manage the fallout, the rest of the Parks Department picks up her workload and gets pulled into their own holiday gift-shopping subplot, trying to track down the perfect presents for the people in their lives.

"Citizen Knope" — Season 4, Episode 10

Suspended from the Parks Department amid a separate controversy, Leslie tries to stay useful by forming an outside citizen action group, since she can't work from within the office anymore. Meanwhile, her coworkers quietly team up to figure out the ideal Christmas gift for her, wanting to do right by someone who's given so much to the department. Ben, in the middle of searching for a private-sector job, gets pulled into the gift-planning chaos too.

"Ron and Diane" — Season 5, Episode 9

Ron gets nominated for the Indiana Fine Woodworking Award, and Leslie insists on tagging along to the ceremony with him and his new girlfriend, Diane, for moral support. The night turns chaotic when Tammy II crashes the event determined to pull Ron back into her orbit, leaving Leslie to run interference while Ron tries to reassure Diane his friendship with Leslie is purely platonic. Back at the office, Tom, April, Andy, and Donna's annual "Jerry Dinner" plans get upended when they discover Jerry is throwing a Christmas party at his house and didn't invite them.
