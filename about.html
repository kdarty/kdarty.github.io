About Me

Hi, I'm Kevin Darty — a Software Engineering Manager with a passion for clean code, creative projects, and strong coffee.

By day, I lead talented development teams working with .NET, C#, and Angular, focusing on building secure, scalable solutions. I enjoy solving tough technical problems, mentoring devs, and finding smart ways to integrate AI into our workflows.

Outside of work, I run a growing T-shirt and merch business where I get to blend my creative side with tech and design. I'm especially drawn to themes like coffee culture, geeky humor, and seasonal vibes — and yes, I do love a good pumpkin spice anything.

I'm also a lifelong guitar player and gear nerd, always exploring tones and tinkering with pedals when I'm not writing code or designing shirts. Music, digital art, and automation tools are all part of how I stay balanced and creative.

Whether it's modernizing legacy code or finding just the right caption for a meme shirt, I enjoy projects that mix logic, creativity, and a bit of fun.
